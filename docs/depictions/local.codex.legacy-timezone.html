Legacy Timezone Update

IANA 2026e timezone data for iOS 4.3–7

IANA 2026e time rules for iOS 4.3-7 on armv7 devices.

Updates system and ICU timezone data, including Russian UTC offsets. Preserves chosen timezone and UTC clock. Removal restores original data.

2026e-2 installed, removed, reinstalled and rebooted on iPad mini2/7.1.2. All600 managed files verify; native armv7 and arm64 NSTimeZone, libc and NSDateFormatter match155 IANA date/region cases each. Moscow isUTC+3 with historical offsets preserved. Removal restores original data. No background service.
