Legacy Timezone Update

IANA 2026e timezone data for iOS 4–6

IANA 2026e time rules for iOS 4.3-6 on armv7 devices.

Updates system and ICU timezone data, including Russian UTC offsets. Preserves chosen timezone and UTC clock. Removal restores original data.

The installer validates the iOS ICU layout and intentionally refuses iOS 7; a separate iOS 7 ICU adapter is still required.

Details

  • Requires: firmware (>= 4.3), firmware (<< 7.0), gsc.armv7
