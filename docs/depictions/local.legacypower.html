Power Logger

Battery and per-process CPU logger for iOS 6–7

Switchable local battery and per-process CPU logger for iOS6-7 (armv7/arm64).

Records signed battery current, charging, process CPU, GPU and traffic. No per-app watt claims, network probes or wake assertions. Bounded logs.

iOS 7.1.2 arm64: installed, launchd started the universal daemon, status.json written. Recording stays off until enabled.
