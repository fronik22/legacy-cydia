Legacy Device Access

One-shot SpringBoard lock/unlock helper

Explicit root-operated no-passcode screen access for device tests.

Version-specific adapters for iOS 4.3.3, 6.1.3 and 7.x. No daemon or boot auto-unlock.

iOS 7.1.2 verified: 1.1.0 performed status, lock and unlock and refused a simulated passcode. One-shot root CLI; no daemon or boot hook. iOS 4.3.3 and 6.1.3 adapters remain.
